***OPEN VIEWING WEDNESDAY 2ND SEP 18:00-18:30***

DNG Rathfarnham are pleased to present 5 Orchardton to the market. This impressive detached four-bedroom family home offers excellent space in a quiet cul-de-sac position opposite a lovely communal green.

A substantial property of approximately 130 square metres 5 Orchardton boasts well laid out accommodation spread across two floors. Entering via the front door into the spacious hallway a staircase rises up to the first-floor accommodation. To the right, a generous lounge/reception room faces out to the front and to the left a large living room boasts a similar outlook. Moving to the rear of the house from the living room via double doors there is a generous dining room with sliding doors to the rear garden and a separate door with direct access to the large kitchen/breakfast room which also benefits from sliding door to the rear garden. Upstairs there are four bedrooms, a generous single, two doubles and a very large master with its own en-suite bathroom. A family bathroom completes the upstairs accommodation, and a large attic above offers great storage and potential for conversion if desired.

Outside to the front of the property is an concrete driveway and attractive low-maintenance garden space with gravel covering and mature planting. A very wide gated side passage leads to the rear garden with a lovely sunny south-easterly aspect. The garden area boasts a solid block-built storage shed and boiler house along with a large patio, raised deck and artificial lawn bordered by mature planted borders.

The property is heated by oil fired central heating and has achieved a D BER rating with A potential. With such a large site there is excellent scope to increase the footprint of the property with an extension to the rear if desired and/or conversion of the spacious attic.

Orchardton is a quiet mature road positioned just off Willowbank Drive close to its junction with Ballyroan Road. Interestingly it is made up of Old Orchardton and New Orchardton with identical numbering for both and this property is in the newer section constructed in the late 1970's. An ideal family development the local area boasts green spaces, parks, sports clubs, and schools at every level. Nearby Rathfarnham Village offers a wealth of services from shops to pubs, restaurants, and banking services. The area is well served by public transport and the M50 motorway is just a short drive away.

A substantial detached four-bedroom family home in a peaceful location with huge potential for those seeking a forever home to mould into their personal style 5 Orchardton has a lot to offer. Viewing of this impressive property is highly recommended and can be arranged with selling agent Dan Steen.
